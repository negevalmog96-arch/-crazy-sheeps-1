          50 sheep · one farm · last one standing wins

          Charge across the farm and ram the other sheep off their feet. When you get hit, push the stick the other way to catch your balance before you blow up.

          • Keyboard: W or arrows to move and turn, Shift to sprint, Space to boost, E to vanish, X to stop, hold Z to aim, V to switch camera, P to pause, Enter to start.
          • Touch (phones without a controller): press and hold anywhere to run, slide your finger left or right to steer, and use SPRINT, BOOST, VANISH, STOP, AIM and VIEW. BOOST and VANISH show a countdown and only work when they are ready.
          • Menus with a controller: R1 and L1 move the highlight right and left, ✕ chooses, ○ goes back. Sliders in the shop move with the left stick or the d-pad.
          • Team vs bots: in a match lobby the host can pick Team vs bots. Real players can't knock each other over, and the team wins when every bot is down (+10 grass each). Names of real players float above their sheep, and you see a message every time a teammate knocks a sheep out.
          • Team vs team: the host picks Team vs team, everyone joins Red or Blue (or gets shared out), and bots fill both teams up to 25. Teammates can't knock each other over and stand on rings in their team colour. Knock out every sheep on the other team to win (+10 grass each). You see who knocked out whom: green for your team, red for theirs.
          • Shields appear around the farm. Pick one up and it blocks the next hit.
          • The farm shrinks slowly during a round, so the fence pushes everyone together.
          • Highlights: after each round you get a video of your kills and how you went out, ready to share.
          • Pause: press △ (or P on a keyboard) in games without other real players.
          • Grass: win +10, 2nd +7, 3rd +5. Two kills within a couple of seconds is a double kill (+2), three is a triple kill (+5). Places 35 to 48 lose 5, second to last loses 5 and last place loses 8. Sign in with Google to earn it.
          • Shop: spend coins on Sheep Colors and Sheep Patterns. Design your own look with stripes and dots; paint is priced by how much of the sheep it covers. Looks are cosmetic only.
          • Coins: every game pays coins. By place: 1st 50, 2nd 35, 3rd 25, top 10 get 15, top 25 get 8, everyone else 3. Team games: win 30, loss 5. 1v1: win 25, loss 5. Plus 5 coins for every kill.
          • Level: every 40 grass raises your level. The higher your level, the faster and smarter the bots get.
          • 1v1: search for a player and challenge them. Best of 3 rounds on a 1v1 farm with five ramps: hit a ramp fast to jump, and land on your rival to knock them over. The winner gets 5 grass.
          • Football mode: press FOOTBALL MODE ⚽ on the home screen. 11 sheep a side, 3:30 matches against bots or with friends (the host shares a room code and picks the teams). You drive your sheep exactly like here: same camera, speed, sprint, boost, vanish and stop. ✕ / F / KICK: tap to pass, hold to shoot. Ram opponents to knock them over; they come back after 3 seconds at their starting spot.
          • Daily missions: three new missions every day (easy, medium, hard), the same for everyone. Each pays coins once (20, 30 or 45). Finish all three for +25 more and +1 day on your streak. Miss a day and the streak starts again.
          • Leaderboards: grass, kills (every kill is a point) and streak (the longest run of days with all daily missions done).
